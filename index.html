Why this split

Push, Pull, Legs, Rest, Upper, Lower — repeating. 93 sets a week — past ~18 hard sets a session, extra volume mostly borrows from the next one instead of adding anything.

Side delts get 10 sets across three days — the cheapest width for a V-taper, and your most under-trained muscle before.

Pull-ups open the Pull day and the Upper day, first, fresh — not buried mid-session.

Deload weeks

Every sixth calendar week, set counts are automatically halved — same weights, less volume. This still follows the calendar, since it's tied to your January date, unlike the daily rotation above.

Bulk, then cut
The honest math

The "V" shows up around 10–12% body fat — genetics decide the shape, the cut decides whether it's visible at all. No amount of ab work substitutes for it.

At 180 cm that's roughly 71–75 kg. Lean bulk to ~85 kg now, cut to ~74 by summer — abs visible around March, the V around May.

Protein levers: soya chunks (~52g/100g dry — best value in an Indian kitchen), eggs, curd, paneer, chicken, fish, whey for the last easy 30g.

Cardio: two easy walks/week while bulking. 8,000 steps/day once cutting — add more only when the scale stalls two weeks straight.
